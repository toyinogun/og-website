I'm Toyin Ogunseinde, a DevOps and infrastructure engineer in the Netherlands. I build infrastructure that is reliable, observable and secure: Kubernetes clusters written entirely as code, delivery pipelines that ship through Git, and monitoring that tells you something is wrong before your users do.

I didn't start here. My degree is in accounting, and my first years in tech were in support, at WP Buffs, then LifterLMS, then Yoast, fixing WordPress and PHP problems across every kind of hosting setup you can imagine. Support taught me the thing I still lean on most: how to find out what is actually broken, explain it plainly, and stay calm while you fix it.

Curiosity about what sat underneath those websites pulled me into infrastructure. I built a homelab with VLANs, a pfSense firewall, Active Directory, Proxmox and proper backups, then moved to the cloud. Today I run my own production cluster on Hetzner Cloud: Talos Linux, Terraform, Argo CD, Cilium, Longhorn, Prometheus and Grafana. It hosts my own products and the tools I use every day. By day I'm a systems administrator at Travix, keeping the infrastructure behind a travel business stable and secure.

Lately I've been building the tooling around that work, mostly in Go and TypeScript: Deployer, a platform that lets AI coding agents deploy what they write safely; ShipSafe, which scores AI written pull requests in CI; and KubeAudit EU, a Kubernetes security audit with NIS2 compliance mapping.

I'm AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner and CompTIA Security+ certified. I work best where I can own a system end to end and work closely with the people who depend on it.
